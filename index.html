About Me

I am interested in designing and studying AI systems that support professional judgment while preserving human agency and independent capability.

My research interests grew from developing DealLens, an evidence-grounded AI decision-support system. I hope to investigate how AI roles shape human judgment, decision authority, and long-term capability, and how adaptive support can strengthen human independence.
